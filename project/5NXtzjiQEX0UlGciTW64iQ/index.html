Project Overview

Bungalow Home is an boutique interior design studio and high-end residential styling brand specializing in warm minimalism, mid-century modern curation, and tailored space transformation. The studio delivers comprehensive, turnkey residential design solutions—spanning architectural furniture sourcing, custom spatial layout planning, and final interior curation to turn empty properties into deeply personalized, signature homes.

This cohesive social media marketing suite was designed and structured to project an elite, aspirational lifestyle across highly visual digital channels. The primary objective was to transition standard, project-completion portfolio photos into a high-converting, narrative-driven social media campaign. The final carousel ecosystem balances high-end interior editorial layouts with human-centric process imagery, creating an organic digital experience tailored for high engagement on platforms like Instagram, Pinterest, and premium lifestyle ad networks.

🎯 Challenge & Strategy

The core challenge centered on standing out in a crowded digital landscape filled with generic, uncurated interior design feeds. High-end clientele are not swayed by standard marketing pitches; they buy into a mood, a philosophy, and an aesthetic standard. To capture this discerning B2B and affluent B2C audience, the social creative suite needed to communicate architectural harmony, tactile material premiumness, and the personalized, hands-on artistry behind the brand.

The overarching strategy centered on an "Editorial Lifestyle Journey" narrative framework that unfolds seamlessly across three intentional social tiles:

  • Tile 01 (The Branding Anchor): The campaign opens with an architectural kitchen layout, utilizing a prominent, centralized geometric monogram overlay (BH / BUNGALOW HOME) to establish immediate brand equity, sophistication, and upscale studio authority right in the feed.

  • Tile 02 (The Philosophical Hook): The middle of the carousel transitions to a beautifully lit dining room, paired with an elegant, conceptual typography hook (BUNGALOW HOME - Refinement, Reimagined). This section focuses strictly on the finished emotional benefit, establishing the studio's unique design philosophy.

  • Tile 03 (The Human Element): The final tile dismantles consumer inertia by introducing the human element behind the transformation. Featuring an active styling shot of a designer placing custom botanicals (From Blank Space to Signature Style), the creative direction subtly demonstrates the meticulous, hands-on craftsmanship that justifies the premium nature of the service.

🎨 Design & Art Direction

✅ Soft Warm Minimalist & Ochre Palette

The visual identity implements a highly curated, desaturated monochromatic color palette consisting of warm creams, sandy beiges, natural oak tones, and muted ochre accents. This deliberate palette choice mimics natural sunlight and warm linen textures, creating a calming, high-end look and feel that instantly makes social feeds feel expansive and luxurious.

✅ High-End Serif & Editorial Typography

The creative direction pairs a tracking-spaced, minimalist architectural serif font for the main brand headings with a clean, light sans-serif for secondary subheadings. This clean, editorial hierarchy mimics luxury design magazines, ensuring text elements feel integrated into the design rather than cluttered over it.

✅ Geometric Monogram & Watermark Layering

A central feature of the visual suite is the bold, abstract geometric line logo that merges the letters 'B' and 'H'. Rendered in a clean white vector style, this emblem floats effortlessly over the initial kitchen photography, establishing a strong, recognizable trademark that retains its high-end look across various screen sizes.

✅ Sculptural Interior Architecture Framing

The photography avoids flat angles in favor of deep, linear perspectives that highlight the organic, curved shapes of the furniture. By capturing arched mirrors, curved dining tables, and rounded accent seating, the art direction subtly reinforces the studio's focus on fluid spatial harmony.

✅ Soft-Focus Cinematic Natural Lighting

The creative direction uses bright, overexposed natural sunlight streaming through floor-to-ceiling windows to create soft shadow patterns. This lighting choice highlights the natural textures of the oak wood grain, stone countertops, and linen fabrics, giving the static social images a live, welcoming atmosphere.

✅ Narrative "Behind-The-Design" Action Capture

The final image introduces a lifestyle portrait of a designer physically staging a living space. By capturing a real moment of styling, the art direction transforms the campaign from a cold gallery of empty rooms into a personal, trustworthy narrative about custom, artisan craftsmanship.
