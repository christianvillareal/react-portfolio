Project Overview

The official corporate brand guidelines for Ninja Hands Cleaning. Operating as a professional commercial and residential cleaning service, the brand coordinates high-efficiency sanitization with meticulous operational precision.

The primary deliverable of this design system sprint was to architect an immutable visual playbook. Organized across 24 technical slide viewports, this document establishes clear rules for core identity usage, typographic selection, color palettes, iconography libraries, corporate stationery, and real-world asset mockups to maintain absolute consistency across all franchise locations and media touchpoints.

🎯 Challenge & Strategy

The core challenge lay in elevating a service-industry business—cleaning and maintenance—into a highly organized, trustworthy corporate identity. Standard cleaning companies frequently use chaotic, multi-colored motifs that lack corporate professionalism. The business needed an identity that felt approachable, highly disciplined, and modern.

To build an authoritative and cohesive identity system, the art strategy maps out "Structured Functional Sections" across seven numbered milestones:

  • 01 Introduction & Who They Are: Establishes the company culture, core pillars, corporate mission, and operational vision statements alongside real-world lifestyle photography of staff members in action.

  • 02 Our Logo, Usage & Misuse: Sets rigid rules for emblem placement. This area defines exclusion zones, minimum production sizing limits, correct presentation across dark backgrounds, and explicitly details layout misuse configurations (such as warped scales or unauthorized color overlays) to preserve asset integrity.

  • 03 Color Usage & Palette Hierarchy: Breaks the corporate color profiles down into clear primary, secondary, and neutral shades, utilizing neat column grids to display specific swatches.

  • 04 Typography & Font Scales: Regulates typesetting protocols across marketing collateral, calling out bold display typefaces for primary titles next to highly legible geometric fonts for long-form body copy.

  • 05 Iconography & Interface Systems: Introduces a customized, solid-vector icon grid detailing specific residential actions, eco-friendly chemical components, and standard utility workflows.

  • 06 Stationery & Corporate Communication: Displays a cohesive corporate design style across tangible corporate materials, mapping official configurations for mailing envelopes and multi-tone business card mockups.

  • 07 Brand In Use / Real-World Execution: Connects the brand assets directly with real-world marketing materials, showcasing logo placement across dynamic mobile vehicle wraps, branded uniform apparel, corporate aprons, and custom chemical spray bottle graphics.

🎨 Design & Art Direction

✅ Corporate Navy, Crisp Cyan, and True Blue Palette

The digital color scheme focuses on cleanliness, safety, and security. A deep, authoritative corporate navy serves as the primary canvas color across cover slides, title separators, and dark backgrounds. This is contrasted against a bright, fresh cyan tone used for secondary accents and vector elements, creating a crisp, sterile corporate mood that perfectly fits a professional cleaning brand.

✅ Heavy Display and Geometric Body Typography

The typographical hierarchy splits roles clearly. Display headers utilize a heavy, high-impact sans-serif font family in uppercase configurations (STATTLICHES) to command immediate structural attention on layout headings and cover pages. This is supported by a clean, highly legible geometric sans-serif for body paragraphs, ensuring technical instructions remain completely readable at small sizes.

✅ Minimal Masked Ninja Motif Illustration

The visual identity incorporates a modern, circular vector silhouette of a masked ninja character face, which is subtly integrated throughout the document as a watermarked background asset. This graphic device creatively plays on the brand name, communicating that the cleaning teams operate with quiet efficiency, speed, and precision.

✅ Symmetrical Grid and Clean Negative Space

The art direction relies heavily on white background space for instructional pages and solid color blocking for major transition dividers. Text columns, graphic mockups, and icon modules are locked to a strict grid system with ample margin cushions, projecting a sense of meticulous order and organizational discipline that mirrors the company's core values.
