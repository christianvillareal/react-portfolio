Project Overview

Adjudicator AI is an AI-powered automotive workflow platform designed to modernize communication and operational efficiency between mechanics, dealerships, and administrators. The platform streamlines the entire repair and diagnostic process by enabling mechanics to capture engine issues directly from mobile devices, while dealership and admin teams manage approvals, diagnostics, and case workflows through intelligent desktop dashboards.

The website was designed and fully developed to position the platform as a modern automotive-tech solution that combines AI assistance, real-time collaboration, and workflow automation into one seamless ecosystem. The project focused on creating a clean, enterprise-level user experience that communicates innovation, reliability, and operational intelligence while maintaining clarity for both technical and non-technical users.

🎯 Challenge & Strategy

The primary challenge was translating a highly technical automotive AI system into a user-friendly digital experience that could clearly communicate its value to multiple audiences, including dealerships, mechanics, and operational managers. Since the platform combines mobile diagnostics, workflow management, AI assistance, and repair communication into a single ecosystem, the website needed to simplify complex processes without losing depth or credibility.

Another major challenge involved balancing enterprise-level professionalism with modern SaaS aesthetics. The platform operates within the automotive service industry, where trust, speed, and workflow clarity are essential. The interface and site structure needed to feel technologically advanced while still remaining intuitive and approachable for dealership teams and repair professionals. Research into automotive AI workflow platforms and dealership automation systems helped inform the visual direction and messaging strategy.

The overall strategy focused on creating a conversion-oriented experience that highlights the platform’s AI-driven workflow capabilities through strong visual hierarchy, structured content flow, and modern automotive-inspired UI design. Clear sectioning, bold typography, and clean dashboard-inspired layouts were used to guide users through the platform’s core features while reinforcing efficiency, intelligence, and operational control. Real-world automotive AI workflow trends also influenced the system-focused presentation and product positioning.

🎨 Design & Art Direction

✅ Modern Automotive SaaS Aesthetic A sleek enterprise-inspired visual direction was used to position the platform as an advanced AI-powered automotive solution while maintaining clarity and professionalism.

✅ Dashboard-Inspired UI System Structured layouts, clean data presentation, and modular content sections were designed to reflect the workflow-driven nature of the platform.

✅ Multi-Platform User Experience The website visually communicates the connection between mobile mechanic workflows and desktop dealership management systems through cohesive interface storytelling.

✅ Conversion-Focused Landing Experience Strong typography hierarchy, strategic CTA placement, and streamlined content structure were implemented to improve engagement and communicate value quickly.

✅ AI-Driven Brand Positioning Visual elements, spacing, and interface styling were crafted to reinforce themes of automation, intelligence, speed, and operational efficiency across the entire experience.

✅ Full Design-to-Development Execution The entire project was handled from Figma UI/UX design through full website development in Wix, ensuring consistency between design vision, responsiveness, and final implementation.
