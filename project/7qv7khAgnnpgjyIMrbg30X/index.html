Project Overview

This project represents an in-depth market research study engineered for a financial services, auto insurance, or corporate wellness entity (branded under the "Real Insurance" insights mark). The campaign is designed to break down the financial, logistical, and psychological impacts of daily travel, tracking the shifting patterns between working from home, utilizing public transit, and driving private vehicles.

The primary objective of this project was to construct a highly unified, modular design system spanning long-form vertical infographics ($9:16$ macro aspect ratio) and targeted data presentation blocks. This structural flexibility allows complex urban mobility analytics to be deployed effortlessly across content marketing funnels, corporate reports, digital journalism, and social platforms, capturing the interest of daily commuters and remote professionals alike.

🎯 Challenge & Strategy

The core challenge of this data design project was to balance heavy, interconnected financial metrics (such as daily, monthly, and yearly outlays) alongside qualitative behavioral insights (such as employee attitudes and workplace habits) without causing visual crowding. When dealing with multiple comparative datasets, layouts can easily lose structure. To solve this, the creative strategy deployed a "Zoned Sequential Mapping & High-Contrast Segmented Funneling" framework, organizing the layout into clear analytical chapters:

  • The Sequential Journey Map: The foundational charts use a winding, illustrative gray vector roadway to visualize expenses. By mapping data points sequentially along a physical path (titled The Cost of Commuting and concluding with a bold It all adds up!), abstract cost totals are turned into an engaging narrative journey.

  • The Macro Financial Audit: The second phase moves into hard, itemized budgeting blocks. Breaking down costs under prominent headers like THE COST OF COMMUTING and mapping figures across daily ($20$), monthly ($164$), and annual ($4,000$) milestones gives users an instant financial reality check on their travel habits.

  • The Public vs. Private Modal Battle: The center of the campaign shifts to direct behavioral comparison (PUBLIC VS PRIVATE). This zone utilizes comparative percentage towers, charting user sentiments and modal choices side-by-side to illustrate the clear trade-offs between convenience and cost.

  • The Hybrid Work Integration: The final section maps structural changes in employment habits (COMMUTING AND WORK DECISIONS). This block bridges lifestyle choices with logistics, utilizing clear data fractions to illustrate work-from-home distributions and employer support trends.

🎨 Design & Art Direction

✅ Monochromatic Plum & Vibrant Accent Palette

The visual identity establishes a highly disciplined, sophisticated color space centered on deep plum purple, pastel magenta, clean white grids, and sharp charcoal gray vectors. Using varying shades of a single dominant purple tone creates a premium, unified editorial aesthetic across all seven layout panels, while bright neon pink or safety green underlines serve as visual entry points to highlight critical percentages.

✅ Isometric Illustration Style

The art direction elevates abstract statistics by embedding detailed isometric architectural elements directly into the charts. From miniature office high-rises and residential houses to modern train stations, bus stops, and highway intersections, these three-dimensional illustrations transform simple data points into recognizable real-world environments.

✅ Bold Sans-Serif Typographic Hierarchy

The typography layout uses a clean, heavy sans-serif typeface to maintain maximum scannability on small screens. Important figures, headers, and callouts leverage a distinct two-tone color arrangement or high-contrast white text masking over solid plum blocks to establish a commanding, easy-to-follow reading rhythm.

✅ Fraction-Driven Data Badges

To turn text-heavy metrics into glanceable details, the layout highlights lifestyle trends using massive, stylized numerical fraction matrices (such as a bold 7 IN 10 WORKING callout). Flanked by minimal line-art vectors of office workers and commuter trains, these cards function as self-contained micro-infographics.

✅ Unified Corporate Branding Baseline

Every panel is anchored by a persistent, minimalist white bottom footer. The right corner features the official corporate logomark alongside a stylized gradient arrow pointing forward, weaving distinct statistical slices into a single, cohesive brand presentation.
