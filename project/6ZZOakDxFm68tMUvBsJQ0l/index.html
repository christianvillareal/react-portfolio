Project Overview

Broken Hill Motor Company – Social Media Campaign Designs is a modern automotive marketing campaign created to strengthen the dealership’s digital presence and increase engagement across social media platforms. The project focuses on visually compelling promotional content that highlights vehicle offers, dealership credibility, and customer trust through clean layouts, bold typography, and conversion-focused messaging. Designed primarily for mobile-first viewing, the campaign balances professional dealership branding with high-impact advertising visuals to capture attention quickly and communicate value instantly.

🎯 Challenge & Strategy

The primary challenge was creating a cohesive set of automotive promotional materials that could remain visually consistent while supporting different marketing objectives such as vehicle promotions, dealership awareness, and pre-owned inventory campaigns. Each design needed to feel bold and attention-grabbing in crowded social media feeds while still maintaining a clean and trustworthy dealership identity.

Another key challenge involved designing content optimized for mobile engagement, ensuring readability, strong visual hierarchy, and fast communication of key promotional messages across multiple social platforms. Since automotive advertising relies heavily on emotional appeal and product presentation, the layouts also needed to showcase vehicles in a premium and aspirational way without overwhelming the viewer with excessive information.

The overall strategy focused on combining impactful automotive imagery, high-contrast typography, and conversion-oriented composition to create social media assets that are both visually engaging and marketing-effective. Consistent branding elements, strategic color usage, and structured content placement were used to strengthen recognition and improve campaign performance across digital channels.

🎨 Design & Art Direction

✅ Bold Automotive Marketing Visuals Dynamic compositions and high-impact vehicle imagery were used to immediately capture attention and establish a strong dealership advertising presence.

✅ Mobile-Optimized Social Layouts All designs were structured for social-first consumption, ensuring readability, balanced spacing, and effective visual hierarchy on mobile devices.

✅ Consistent Dealership Branding Unified typography, color systems, and branding elements were applied across every campaign material to reinforce professionalism and brand recognition.

✅ Conversion-Focused Composition Promotional messaging, vehicle placement, and callout sections were strategically organized to guide viewer attention and improve engagement performance.

✅ Modern Automotive Aesthetic A sleek and professional visual direction combining contemporary dealership marketing trends with clean graphic execution was used to communicate trust, quality, and reliability.
