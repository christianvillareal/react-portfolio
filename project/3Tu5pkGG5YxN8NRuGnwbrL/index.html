Project Overview

Oakley Straw is an agricultural brand dedicated to supplying premium-grade forage and organic garden enhancements to horticulturists and local growers. Their flagship product, Pea Straw Natural Garden Mulch, is an all-natural, highly nutritive organic layer sourced directly from Victorian farms to shield plant roots, regulate thermal soil dynamics, and boost soil health.

The goal of this packaging design initiative was to step away from the uninspired, clear plastic wrapping typically used for bulk gardening supplies. Instead, the brand needed a premium, high-fidelity sack design that would command premium shelf space in boutique nurseries and commercial garden centers alike.

🎯 Challenge & Strategy

The primary packaging challenge was changing consumer perception. Mass-market mulch is frequently treated as a cheap, low-value commodity. Consumers often buy whatever is closest or cheapest because standard industrial plastic bags fail to communicate product quality, origin, or environmental benefits.

To elevate the brand into a premium tier, the packaging layout applies a "Rustic Heritage & Transparent Utility" structure across the front and back layouts:

  • Establishing Visual Trust and Origin: The face layout replaces generic product photography with a detailed, hand-drawn botanical illustration of pea pods and winding vines. This intricate line work immediately communicates care, biological purity, and premium quality.

  • A Highly Readable Benefit Matrix: Instead of hiding product benefits in dense paragraphs, the front panel uses a clean, split-grid layout. Key product actions—such as moisture retention, erosion control, and weed prevention—are organized into highly scannable bullet lists.

  • Two-Tone Information Architecture: The packaging options alternate between a striking dark-mode forest green and a rustic, weathered cream palette. This intentional color blocking separates initial branding elements from the functional, step-by-step application instructions on the reverse panel.

🎨 Design & Art Direction

✅ Earthy Forest Green and Rich Cream Palette

The primary color scheme balances a deep, organic forest green with a warm, natural cream background. This combination mirrors healthy foliage and rich soil, allowing the product to look right at home in greenhouse settings while subtly hinting at its eco-friendly, biodegradable materials.

✅ High-Contrast Woodblock Typography

The product title, PEA STRAW, is set in a bold, weathered sans-serif typeface that feels hand-stamped. This heavy woodblock weight gives the brand a rugged, farm-fresh feel, while secondary headings use a clean, geometric sans-serif to keep the instructions highly readable.

✅ Fine-Line Botanical Asset Art

The illustrations feature meticulous anatomical details on the leaves, pods, and stems. This delicate line art provides a elegant contrast against the thick, bold block typography, striking a perfect balance between rugged farm utility and refined gardening luxury.

✅ Clean, Functional Layout Grid

The reverse side avoids cluttered, overlapping blocks by relying on clean vertical lists, minimal dividing lines, and clear circular icons. This layout maps out the preparation, watering, and laying steps in a clean timeline, proving that high-end packaging can look elegant while remaining highly functional.
