Project Overview

City Motors is an automotive dealership brand specializing in premium new vehicle sales, certified pre-owned vehicles, and high-performance multi-utility trucks. Operating across retail and digital spaces, the brand provides comprehensive dealership solutions—including vehicle trade-ins, dealership financing, and direct-to-consumer digital automotive marketing.

This social media marketing suite was designed and structured to elevate City Motors' digital presence across modern feeds. The primary objective was to transform standard dealership inventory displays into high-converting, premium social creative assets. The final campaign balances clean, high-impact typography with strategic mobile UI positioning, delivering a multi-platform design ecosystem optimized for high click-through rates (CTR) on platforms like Instagram, Facebook, and targeted mobile ad networks.

🎯 Challenge & Strategy

The core challenge centered on creating visual assets that stand out in highly competitive, fast-scrolling social media feeds. Automotive buyers looking for their next vehicle face immense choice paralysis. Standard dealership social graphics often feel cluttered, look identical, or lack clear brand recall. The creative assets needed to project immediate authority, clearly categorize vehicle types, and provide a frictionless psychological path from a user scrolling their feed to a warm website visitor.

The overarching strategy centered on a "Feed-Stopping Performance" narrative framework. The campaign splits into specialized, highly targeted design pathways. To capture broad top-of-funnel traffic, the initial creatives lead with direct, bold typography hooks (YOUR NEW CAR AWAITS AT CITY MOTORS!) paired with high-end vehicle tracking imagery to establish scale.

To capture high-intent pre-owned buyers, the strategy shifts to a structured grid layout clearly calling out product quality (HIGH QUALITY PRE-OWNED VEHICLES!). This pathing ensures that someone searching for an upgrade and a buyer seeking a rugged, work-ready utility truck can lock onto relevant inventory immediately.

To dismantle final digital friction, the final stages of the funnel implement an app-in-app aesthetic. By nesting these high-impact graphics inside a premium smartphone mock-up user interface, the creative direction seamlessly mimics native user behavior, creating a psychological bridge that encourages users to tap through to the direct digital catalog.

🎨 Design & Art Direction

✅ High-Velocity Corporate Red & Neon Green Palette

The visual identity implements a strong, high-contrast palette pairing an aggressive corporate red and a bright neon green accent color against deep charcoal, black, and white foundations. This color combination directly references performance sports cars and active hazard lights, creating an immediate psychological link to automotive power, agility, and premium performance.

✅ Multi-Window Inventory Framing

The art direction cuts through flat social posts by utilizing a dynamic, asymmetric image layout. By stacking live dealership interactions (such as handshake handovers and professional mechanics in service bays) alongside main vehicle profiles, the design builds trust and shows the comprehensive team support behind every purchase.

✅ Stark Block-Level Typography

The creative direction uses thick, high-impact sans-serif typography stacked in clear reading blocks. This choice ensures that key brand elements (DISCOVER YOUR NEW CAR AT CITY MOTORS!) remain fully legible on small mobile screens, even when users are scanning down their timelines at high speeds.

✅ Cinematic Outdoor Asset Placements

The vehicle cutouts drop traditional, boring studio backgrounds in favor of immersive outdoor environments. Placing premium family SUVs in vast desert sand dunes and rugged utility trucks on mountain trails subtly matches the vehicle models to the exact aspirational lifestyles and terrains their drivers desire.

✅ App-Inspired Interface Mockups

To maximize conversions on mobile-first ad channels, the final design variants wrap the imagery inside a realistic smartphone mockup container. This design layer creates a familiar, native interaction point that shortens the path from a casual social view to active online exploration.

✅ Consistent Corporate Multi-Branding

Every creative asset across the funnel is anchored by a clean, uniform placement of the CITY MOTORS corporate logotype and secondary trust identifiers at the bottom edges. This strict composition layout guarantees strong brand recall across ad sets and organic posts alike, leaving a memorable impression on prospective buyers.
