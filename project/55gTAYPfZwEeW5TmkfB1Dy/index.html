Project Overview

Adjudicator AI – Mechanic App Designs is a multi-platform automotive workflow system designed to streamline communication between mechanics, dealerships, and administrators. The platform enables mechanics to capture and submit real-time engine issue documentation via mobile, while dealership and admin users manage approvals, diagnostics, and case tracking through desktop dashboards. An integrated AI assistant supports diagnosis and decision-making, improving speed, accuracy, and operational efficiency across the entire repair ecosystem.

🎯 Challenge & Strategy

The primary challenge was designing a unified system that seamlessly connects three distinct user environments—mobile mechanics in the field, dealership operators managing repair workflows, and administrators overseeing system-wide operations. Each role required a different level of complexity, yet all needed to remain part of a cohesive ecosystem.

A key focus was enabling real-time visual communication between mechanics and dealerships through image-based issue reporting, ensuring that engine problems could be accurately captured, transmitted, and interpreted without delays or miscommunication. On top of this, the integration of an AI assistant introduced the need for a structured yet intuitive workflow that could support decision-making without overwhelming users.

The overall strategy centered on simplifying complex repair processes through role-specific interfaces, reducing friction in communication, and enhancing operational speed through automation and AI-assisted insights.

🎨 Design & Art Direction

✅ Role-Based Interface Architecture

Separate but connected UI systems were designed for Admin, Dealership, and Mechanic users, each optimized for their specific workflows and data needs.

✅ Clarity-Driven Dashboard Design

Admin and dealership screens prioritize structured data visualization, enabling fast decision-making through clean hierarchy and simplified analytics.

✅ Mobile-First Field Experience

The mechanic interface is optimized for real-world use, focusing on quick camera capture, minimal input steps, and fast submission of diagnostic reports.

✅ Professional Automotive Aesthetic

A clean, functional visual style was used across all platforms, combining modern enterprise UI principles with subtle automotive-tech styling to communicate reliability and precision.
