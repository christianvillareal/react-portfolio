Project Overview

This creative design sprint was engineered for a casual-premium Korean restaurant concept specializing in high-energy, authentic communal dining experiences (branded under a modern "Korean Hotpot" service mark). The menu campaign aims to promote a flagship signature group meal combo featuring premium twists on traditional street food favorites, specifically balancing a massive, savory hotpot stew with individualized stone-bowl rice dishes and casual side platters.

The primary objective was to design a highly appetizing, high-conversion point-of-sale (POS) menu poster and table tent insert optimized for vertical publishing formats ($3:4$ aspect ratio). The promotional design serves as a visual ordering guide—grouping a complex multi-dish culinary bundle into a singular, undeniable dining option. The final variations establish a vibrant, flavor-forward aesthetic that drives high-margin combo orders during busy lunch and dinner services.

🎯 Challenge & Strategy

The core menu design challenge layout centered on effectively displaying multiple intersecting menu items—a central hotpot, a rice bowl, a noodle side dish, and grilled skewers—in a single layout without making the page look cluttered or confusing to a hungry customer. When a combo menu features too many moving parts, diners experience choice paralysis.

The overarching creative strategy deployed a "Visual Feast Hierarchy & Multi-Sensory Appetite Hook" framework, methodically guiding the customer from curiosity to checkout across four distinct design variations:

  • The Signature Hero Anchor: Every layout anchors the diner's eye with a central, steaming bowl of Korean bibimbap topped with a perfect sunny-side-up egg. Surrounding this central dish are secondary platters, creating a natural circular layout that shows off the variety and portion size of the entire combo meal at a glance.

  • The Clear Ingredient Callout: The headlines use direct, descriptive wording (KING PRAWN ARMY STEW & UNAGI BULGOGI BEEF BAP) to highlight premium, high-value ingredients like king prawns, unagi (eel), and bulgogi beef. This helps justify the premium price of the combo right away.

  • The Micro-Platter Inventory: The lower half of the variations breaks down the accompanying side dishes using clean vector pointer lines. Explicitly calling out elements like Cheesy Tteokbokki (rice cakes), Grilled Unagi, and Cheesy Tteok-Gguchi ensures customers know exactly what sides are included in their bundle.

  • The Dietary & Legal Trust Badges: To appeal to a wider audience, the footer area incorporates clear, professional indicators. Placing a verified Halal certification seal alongside official social handles and a crisp website address turns a promotional poster into a trustworthy, professional menu card.

🎨 Design & Art Direction

✅ High-Saturation Searing & Deep Charcoal Palette

The visual identity transitions between two distinct color worlds across its design variations to test different dining atmospheres. Variants 1, 2, and 4 utilize a dark, texturized charcoal slate backdrop lined with fresh, vivid green lettuce leaves to recreate the authentic feel of a premium Korean BBQ table setup. In contrast, Variant 3 implements a bright cobalt blue and crimson red color block, pulling inspiration directly from the South Korean flag to project an energetic, modern street-food vibe.

✅ Expressive Brush Script & Block Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a dynamic pairing of styles to emphasize flavor and excitement. Main menu hooks utilize an organic, textured brush script font (King Prawn Army Stew) that brings a rustic, hand-cooked energy to the page. This is balanced by a bold, clean, extra-thick block sans-serif typeface for the core proteins (BEEF BAP), maximizing readability for quick reading in low-lit restaurant interiors.

✅ Top-Down Floating Food Photography

The art direction treats the culinary elements through high-resolution, perfectly lit food styling. Individual dishes are captured from a slightly elevated three-quarter view, preserving the rich textures of the grilled meats, glossy sauces, and crisp garnishes. Soft drop shadows beneath each plate elevate the food off the flat background, giving the items an appetizing, three-dimensional depth.

✅ Intuitive Vector Pointer Labels

To handle multi-item descriptions cleanly, the design uses thin white indicator lines paired with custom font descriptions. These pointers trace directly from the side platters to simple text labels, acting as an easy visual map that lets diners browse the different sides without getting bogged down by a wall of text.

✅ Clean High-Contrast Footer Callouts

The menu templates wrap up with a solid, high-visibility bottom banner. Keeping this area free of distracting food images allows key operational details—like social media icons, pricing notes, and delivery logos—to sit neatly on one line, ensuring customers can easily find the restaurant's digital channels.
