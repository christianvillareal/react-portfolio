Project Overview

This Project represents a premium consumer research campaign engineered for a financial services provider, insurance group, or family planning agency (branded under the "Choosi" financial insights mark). The campaign translates dense sociological metrics regarding the real-world expenses and behavioral shifts linked to parenthood into highly engaging micro-content.

The primary objective of this project was to establish an intuitive, visually accessible layout framework across long vertical infographics ($9:16$ macro aspect ratio) and modular slide panels. This structure optimizes complex statistics for digital publishing, email newsletters, and content marketing funnels, effectively capturing the attention of millennial couples, young professionals, and growing families navigating long-term wealth management.

🎯 Challenge & Strategy

The core data design challenge lay in organizing multifaceted comparative statistics—spanning percentiles, budget categories, and emotional lifestyle sacrifices—without creating cognitive overload or messy data clusters. Financial planning data can quickly look cold and intimidating. To solve this, the visual strategy deployed a "Zoned Data Layering & Friendly Demographic Contextualization" framework, breaking the informational architecture into distinct analytical phases:

  • The Emotional Sacrifice Hook: The introductory panels open with a striking question badge: WHAT ARE WE WILLING TO SACRIFICE TO AFFORD RAISING CHILDREN?. By mapping lifestyle trade-offs (such as career adjustments or reduced disposable income) across side-by-side comparative bars, the design immediately draws parents and couples into a relatable narrative.

  • The Macro Financial Reality Check: The middle phase introduces direct budget data under high-impact headings like Cost of Kids Sticker Shock and THE COST OF KIDS ADDS UP. This section details cost trajectories—ranking top expenses alongside highly underestimated hidden outlays—giving families a direct reality check on early child-rearing.

  • Micro-Expense Milestone Auditing: To make the macro numbers relatable, the detailed data slides segment costs into real-world milestones (BABYHOOD AND SHOWERS, BIRTHDAYS AND CELEBRATIONS, POCKET MONEY). By pairing specific itemized actions (like baby showers versus actual milestone birthday parties) with comparative blue and orange metric bars, the layout transforms abstract financial projections into daily realities.

🎨 Design & Art Direction

✅ High-Trust Navy & Action-Oriented Orange Palette

The color architecture relies on an intentional contrast strategy tailored to financial services. The foundation uses a deep, trustworthy midnight navy blue to convey security, analytical precision, and institutional authority. This professional canvas is punctuated by bright safety orange header panels and vibrant cerulean blue data pills, establishing a clear visual hierarchy that guides the eye to important statistical findings.

✅ Two-Tone Comparative Data Bars

To display split demographics cleanly, the layout introduces a uniform, dual-color data-masking system. For instance, in the sacrifice charts, the statistics are split into custom columns (COUPLES PLANNING in blue versus PARENTS PLANNING in orange). This clean contrast allows readers to mentally filter and track shifting trends across complex data clusters instantly.

✅ Illustrative Demographic Icons

The art direction anchors the analytical charts using approachable, friendly vector character illustrations. Depicting a diverse family timeline—from parents holding a newborn to growing children playing—the visual language replaces cold, clinical spreadsheet styling with warm, human-centric storytelling that fits the lifestyle context perfectly.

✅ Fragmented Metric Blocks & Clean Borders

To maximize scannability, the layout breaks dense numbers into individual text-masking boxes outlined by crisp white borders. Percentages and cost indicators are accompanied by minimal line-art vectors representing everyday household categories, turning numbers into a fast-moving, highly visual menu.
