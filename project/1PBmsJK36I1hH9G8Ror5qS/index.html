Project Overview

This brand development and product packaging suite centers on a high-volume snack food and pantry goods line engineered for modern retail networks and digital delivery marketplaces. The project highlights complete brand design overhauls for two flagship food product lines: Super Crisp Potato Chips (featuring artisanal canister cylinder packaging) and Super Tasty Processed Peas (featuring classic metal tin can architectures).

The primary objective of this project layout was to develop a scalable corporate packaging template suite. The design architecture must translate seamlessly across flat manufacturing die-cut layouts, standalone retail tubes, traditional tin cans, and localized e-commerce listing graphics. The final assets bridge the gap between vintage food illustration style and modern minimalist commercial formatting, ensuring striking on-shelf identity and instantly recognizable digital thumbnails.

🎯 Challenge & Strategy

The core packaging challenge centered on balancing high-impact shelf appetite appeal with a structured information layout that remains perfectly uniform across entirely different packaging shapes (cylindrical tubes versus short tin cylinders). Food packaging relies on instant visual communication; consumers decide on snack purchases in fractions of a second based on color saturation and clear asset recognition. If a layout is text-heavy or uses uninspired product illustrations, it fails to clear retail hurdles.

The overarching design strategy deployed a "Variant Color-Coding & Ingredient Spotlighting" framework, methodically dividing the food products into distinct visual categories:

  • The High-Velocity Snack Canister Line: To market the premium potato chips line, the design implements a classic stacked structure. The layouts isolate a crisp, high-end 3D render of the food product at the focal core, framed dynamically by fresh botanical seasoning leaves and floating ingredients (like a stylized vinegar clay jar or fresh chili vectors) to broadcast exact flavor categories instantly.

  • The Pantry Staples Tin Line: To handle the processed greens category, the framework transitions to a fresh, health-centric layout. By showcasing a rustic basket spilling over with natural pea pods directly above a textured heap of loose peas, the imagery elevates a simple canned vegetable into an appetizing, ready-to-eat kitchen component.

  • Flat Print-to-Object Blueprint Calibration: To guarantee flawless commercial manufacturing output, the design suite stress-tests flat digital die-lines directly alongside real-world 3D object mockups. Pairing flat label dimensions (complete with nutrition facts, ingredient lists, barcodes, and net-weight metrics) with realistic product renders allows developers to review text wrapping and edge bleed lines before moving to large-scale label production.

🎨 Design & Art Direction

✅ Appetite-Stimulating Flavor Palette

The art direction anchors each food product variant with a custom, highly saturated color scheme engineered to trigger rapid flavor association:

  • The Salt & Vinegar Variant: Employs a crisp, warm cream background paired with warm orange-brown framing elements to communicate a classic, savory crunch.

  • The Hot Chili Variant: Uses a bold, high-energy gradient blending deep fire red with warm orange backdrops, accented by a bright green pepper vector to instantly cue heat and intense spice.

  • The Processed Peas Line: Implements a fresh, monochromatic lime and forest green scheme, using a patterned background of repeating pea vector shapes to emphasize natural freshness and garden-to-table quality.
