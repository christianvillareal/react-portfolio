Project Overview

CHIKABONITA is a premium bohemian-chic fashion label and direct-to-consumer (DTC) e-commerce apparel brand specializing in airy dresses, curated seasonal wardrobe pieces, and resort-wear styling. Tailored for style-conscious women, the brand delivers highly aesthetic, free-spirited garments designed to transition effortlessly from daily luxury to vacation wear.

This comprehensive social media promotional suite was designed and structured to drive rapid customer acquisition and inventory turnover during a high-impact seasonal clearance event. The primary objective was to convert a massive site-wide promotion into an organized, feed-stopping social media campaign. The final carousel framework balances premium bohemian lifestyle photography with clear e-commerce conversion triggers, establishing an interactive ad suite optimized for high click-through rates (CTR) on platforms like Instagram and Facebook.

🎯 Challenge & Strategy

The core challenge centered on presenting a deep promotional discount without diminishing the brand's premium boutique equity. Mass sales can frequently make a luxury lifestyle brand appear cheap if the graphics look too aggressive or clinical. The campaign needed to explicitly shout out a compelling value proposition while retaining the soft, editorial, and aspirational mood that drives apparel purchases.

The overarching strategy centered on a "High-Affinity Promotional Funnel" framework that builds excitement through a multi-tile story arc across five distinct assets:

  • Tile 01 (The Aspirational Opening): The journey hooks the user with a clean, indoor lifestyle portrait. It introduces a soft, muted banner layer displaying the core promotional hook (50% OFF! YOUR DREAM WARDROBE IS HERE) underneath a clean, minimalist brand stamp to immediately establish high-value curiosity.

  • Tile 02 (The Urgency & Exception Clearer): The second tile moves to a bright outdoor close-up, expanding the offer to full-scale sitewide urgency (50% OFF! Everything). Crucially, it uses clean micro-copy underneath to clear up pre-purchase friction and handle exceptions transparently (EXCEPT PRE-ORDERS & BONITA BABY).

  • Tile 03 (The Grid Lookbook): To convert initial interest into a direct purchase craving, the middle tile transitions to a multi-window lifestyle collage showcasing a variety of outfit use-cases. This tile re-anchors the discount against an action-oriented style claim (YOUR NEW FAVORITE STYLES!).

🎨 Design & Art Direction

✅ Earthy Sun-Drenched & Terracotta Palette

The visual identity implements a highly curated, desaturated bohemian color scheme consisting of soft terracotta pinks, warm creams, sandy neutrals, and light olive greens. This earthy palette mimics Mediterranean stone textures and warm sunlight, letting the vibrant colors of the dresses stand out while ensuring the overall feed feels calm and upscale.

✅ Clean Capsule Watermark Identification

Brand presence is maintained across every single slide via a pristine, rounded capsule logo placeholder situated at the top center (CHIKABONITA). This minimalist, high-contrast label ensures strong brand recall without obstructing the flowing textures of the apparel photography.

✅ Translucent Overlay Banners

The core call-to-action text elements are housed within solid, muted taupe and pink horizontal bar overlays. By anchoring the promotional text (50% OFF!) to these dedicated color bands, the design guarantees perfect text legibility over busy floral patterns and detailed background stone walls.

✅ Asymmetric Lookbook Grid Layout

Tile 03 incorporates an elegant, asymmetric layout grid that splits the frame between a full-length styling shot and two stacked micro-previews. This structural format gives users a rapid, glanceable lookbook experience that hints at a diverse and expansive product collection.

✅ Native Smartphone Interface Layering

The final two stages of the ad suite utilize a premium dark-mode setting to background a realistic smartphone UI mockup. Wrapping the lookbook images within a familiar social feed layout transforms the ad into a native-feeling shopping hub, effectively driving up swipe-up conversions.

✅ Human-Centric Bohemian Storytelling

The art direction prioritizes authentic human emotion over stiff studio poses. By capturing candid smiles, natural hair movement, and lifestyle accessorizing (like wide-brimmed hats and white western boots), the campaign successfully sells a relaxed, adventurous lifestyle rather than just individual clothing pieces.
